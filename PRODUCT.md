# Q Workwear

## Product truth

Q Workwear is a Kigali workwear and uniform business owned by Quddus and Tinah. The website helps business buyers browse corporate, hospitality, industrial and branded clothing, then request an order-specific quotation. It is not a shop with fixed stock, cart or checkout.

## Brief

Build the strongest complete product from the available brief; the clients refine it to taste. The client reference is Afrikanda Holdings / Exotiká. Adopt clear collection-led browsing and an uncluttered showcase, not unverified claims about that reference.

The requested experience is cinematic, simple and photography-led: no ornamental tailoring metaphors, badges, stacked labels, public editorial comments or filler copy. Five images—one craft image and four collection images—will be supplied or generated through Kaze’s GPT subscription. Missing final imagery does not block layout implementation.

## Content boundaries

- Business name, owners, city and four collection areas come from the provided project context.
- `qworkwearsolution@gmail.com` is the business contact provided in the conversation; outbound delivery is not yet tested.
- Phone and WhatsApp numbers are not confirmed. Hide these channels until valid approved numbers are configured.
- Do not invent certified protection, fabric weights, production capacity, order minimums, delivery guarantees, payment terms, founder biographies, client names or completed orders.
- `/work` is a collection lookbook, not a fictional portfolio. Illustrative imagery must not imply real commissions or specific premises.
- A final-looking website is not permission to turn unknowns into facts. Keep production verification notes in internal documentation, not visitor-facing banners.

## Implementation constraints

Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript and Tailwind; static export suitable for Vercel or Cloudflare Pages. No CMS, database, authentication, checkout or recurring infrastructure is required. Quote enquiries open the visitor’s mail application; no server submission or delivery confirmation is implied. Preserve enquiry context between collection browsing and contact.

## Acceptance

All five routes render at mobile and desktop sizes. The menu supports keyboard access and focus return. Collection filters and enquiry context work. No broken image links, horizontal overflow or invented commercial claims. Typecheck, production build and browser checks provide verification evidence. Client approval, final image suitability, email/WhatsApp delivery and public deployment require separate confirmation.
