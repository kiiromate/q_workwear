## Tracking scheme

| Asset ID | Deck slide | Sector | Visible subject / original label | Original file / source | Q-produced? | Publication consent | Visual class | Approved caption | Destination | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TBD | TBD | TBD | not visually inspected | TBD | unknown | unknown | undetermined | pending | pending | BLOCKED |

Use these exact statuses:
- `INVENTORIED`: visually inspected and original identified; not automatically publishable.
- `PENDING_PERMISSION`: actual source/rights not yet established.
- `APPROVED_CONCEPT`: permission exists to show as a **generic example**; clearly label as illustrative.
- `APPROVED_REAL_WORK`: explicit permission and evidence it is actually Q Workwear's completed work.
- `REJECTED`: inaccurate, low quality, third-party rights unclear or unsuitable.

Uninspected/unknown rows default to **BLOCKED**. Do not copy ambiguous images into `public/`. Staging paths and original sources should be recorded, not committed if they contain private third-party/customer content.
