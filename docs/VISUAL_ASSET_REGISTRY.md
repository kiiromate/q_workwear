## State machine

- `IDEA`: prompt only, no image exists.
- `CANDIDATE`: produced/obtained, needs realism and rights inspection.
- `REJECTED`: failed garment/brand/rights/crop review; retain reason.
- `EDITED`: image composited with exact Q logo, needs review.
- `SELECTED_INTERNAL`: passed automated + visual realism QA and integrated into branch preview; awaits owner final sign-off and Q/Tinah client approval (`business_approval: PENDING_CLIENT_SIGNOFF`).
- `APPROVED_PUBLIC`: written authorization from relevant image rights holder and owner, visual/copy QA passed.
- `BLOCKED_RIGHTS`: permissions unclear or third-party trademarks present (e.g., `docs/BUSINESS PORTFOLIO.pptx` Slide 40 client logos and unverified stock/catalog cutouts on Slides 8–39).
- `ARCHIVED_ABSTRACT`: replaced geometric PIL prototype image, retired from production.
