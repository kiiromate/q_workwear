## What Photos to Take (Smartphone Photos are Great!)

You do not need an expensive camera to get started. High-resolution smartphone photos taken in good natural light work beautifully.

### 1. Workshop & Craftsmanship (The Atelier Table)
- **Cutting Table:** Wide shots and top-down shots of your pattern paper, fabric shears, chalk marks, and measuring tapes on the table.
- **Tailors at Work:** Hands cutting fabric, guiding fabric under sewing machines, or ironing seams.
- **Craftsmanship Details:** Close-up shots of double stitching, bar-tacks on pockets, buttonholes, and collar construction.

### 2. Finished Garments on Hangers or Mannequins
- Front and back shots of finished uniforms:
  - Chef coats and aprons
  - Executive shirts and blazers
  - Heavy overalls and reflective jackets
  - Branded polo shirts and caps
- Natural light is best—hang the garments against a clean, neutral wall or mannequin near a window.

### 3. Embroidery & Custom Branding
- Crisp close-ups of embroidered logos, company names, or crests on chest pockets, sleeves, or hats.

### 4. Real Client Teams (If Permitted)
- Photos of any client staff wearing your uniforms in their workplace (e.g., hotel staff, kitchen crews, corporate teams, security teams).
